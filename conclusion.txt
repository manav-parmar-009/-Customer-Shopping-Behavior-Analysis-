Project Analysis

I analysed 10000 records with 20 columns related to customer shopping behaviour.

First I checked the dataset and found no missing values or duplicate records. The average customer age is around 37 years and the average income is around 68.75K.

I analysed customers based on loyalty tiers and found that Bronze has the highest number of customers. Platinum customers have the highest average previous spending.

I also analysed traffic sources. Organic traffic brings the highest number of customers. Email has the highest average order value.

In region wise analysis I found that the South region has the highest number of customers and purchases.

I compared the Control group with Discount10 and FreeShip. The Discount10 group has the highest purchase rate.

I also created graphs to understand customer distribution and weekly purchase trends.

Key Insights

- Bronze has the highest number of customers.
- Platinum customers have the highest average previous spending.
- Organic traffic brings the most customers.
- Email has the highest average order value.
- The South region has the highest number of customers and purchases.
- Discount10 has the highest purchase rate at around 14.42%.
- The Control group has a purchase rate of around 12.76%.
- The FreeShip group has a purchase rate of around 13.31%.
- Customer satisfaction is almost similar across all loyalty tiers.
- Customer purchases change across different weeks.

Conclusion

From this project I understood how to analyse customer shopping behaviour using Python.

I used Pandas for data cleaning and analysis and Matplotlib for creating graphs.

The analysis shows that customer behaviour changes based on loyalty tier and traffic source and experiment group.

Discount10 performed better in purchase rate compared to the other two groups in this dataset.

Overall this project helped me improve my practical knowledge of data cleaning and exploratory data analysis and data visualization.
